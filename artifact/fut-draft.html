Build a squad the way FUT Draft does it: every choice is five cards, and you keep one.

  1. Choose a formation, then your captain.
  2. Fill all 11 positions on the pitch.
  3. Pick 7 substitutes and 5 reserves.
  4. Chase 33 chemistry, then play four knockout matches.
